# Some Precious 



## Overview


Between 2017 and 2020, following a week in the hospital due to an infected foot, I created a series of A5-sized zines, collecting thoughts, observations, and tribulations. Most were produced in very limited editions, at nearby photocopy-shop. Some were never completed.
